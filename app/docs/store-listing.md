Make images and video in the same app — on your device through the AI

BRING YOUR OWN AI
Use an API key (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, GLM, or any
custom endpoint) — or sign in with a subscription you already pay for
(SuperGrok / X Premium+, MiniMax, Kimi). Your prompts go straight from
your device to your provider. Nowhere else.

• Publishing goes to your own GitHub, hosted free on GitHub Pages.
• Free and open source (Apache-2.0): github.com/kerpopule/vibexstudio

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• Builds keep streaming in the background and notify you when done.
• Run several projects at once; hop between them while they build.
• A Lovable-style desk on iPad: chat on the left, live preview on the right.
