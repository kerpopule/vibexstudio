**Description**:
Describe the app you want. Watch it become real.

VibeXStudio is a vibe-coding studio in your pocket: chat with AI, and it
writes the files, renders a live preview, and keeps the whole project on
your device. Publish to your own GitHub and share your app with a link.

NOW FEATURING MEDIA LAB
Make images and video in the same app with the AI providers you connect, or
pair your own Media Lab server for the full studio: cinematic video, music,
characters, and Sparky the director.

BRING YOUR OWN AI
Use an API key (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, GLM, or any
custom endpoint), or sign in with a subscription you already pay for
(SuperGrok / X Premium+, MiniMax, Kimi). Direct connections send prompts
from your device to the provider you select. If you intentionally accept a
Private VibeX invite, VibeXStudio routes prompts and generated output through the disclosed private broker and its upstream model after showing a review-before-connect notice.

YOURS, ACTUALLY
• No mandatory VibeXStudio account.
• Local-first, with no ads, analytics, or telemetry.
• Projects stay local; export and import `.vibex` files through Files or AirDrop.
• Publishing goes to your own GitHub, hosted free on GitHub Pages.
• Free and open source (Apache-2.0): github.com/kerpopule/vibexstudio

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• Builds keep streaming in the background and notify you when done.
• Run several projects at once; hop between them while they build.
• A Lovable-style desk on iPad: chat on the left, live preview on the right.
